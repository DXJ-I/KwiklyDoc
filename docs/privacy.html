Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. The same policy is in the app under Settings → About → Privacy policy.

At a glance

Account or sign-inNone
Data collected by the developerNone. There is no KwiklyDoc server.
Tracking, ads or analyticsNone
Where statements are processedOn your device, unless you choose an AI option and allow it
Optional services that may receive statement textApple Private Cloud Compute; OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or Anthropic with your own API key

Summary

KwiklyDoc converts bank statements you choose into transactions. The developer of KwiklyDoc does not run a server for the app and does not receive, collect, sell or share your documents or personal data. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Processing on your device

Text extraction, OCR, the built-in statement parser and Apple's on-device model run on your iPhone or iPad. Imported copies, exports, conversion history, review notes, approved corrections and the edit audit trail are stored only on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection and excluded from device backups. Temporary copies of documents and exports are deleted automatically.

When statement text leaves your device

Only if you choose a cloud AI option and allow it, the text extracted from a statement — which can include your name, account details, balances and transactions — is sent directly from your device to the service you selected, solely to organise it into transactions:

  • Apple Private Cloud Compute (Apple Intelligence on Apple’s servers). Apple uses the text only to fulfil the request and doesn’t store it or make it accessible to anyone, including Apple. Requests count towards your daily Apple Intelligence allowance;
  • OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or Anthropic, using an API key you provide. Each handles the text under its own privacy policy and retention terms.

KwiklyDoc asks for your permission separately for each service before the first time text is sent, and you can withdraw it any time in Settings → Privacy. CSV, OFX, QFX, QBO and QIF files are always processed on your device.

API keys

API keys you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device and sent only to the matching provider.

Face ID and app lock

If you turn on app lock, iOS verifies Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. KwiklyDoc never receives biometric data.

Your choices and deletion

Use on-device processing to keep all statement text on your device. Settings lets you clear conversion history and the audit trail, or erase all KwiklyDoc data (optionally including API keys). Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Data already sent to an AI service is subject to that service's retention policy.

Children

KwiklyDoc is not directed at children.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published with new versions of the app and on the KwiklyDoc privacy page.
