How it checks your figures

Most converters guess which way money moved. KwiklyDoc works it out from the balances on the page, and tells you when it can’t.

  1. Import

    Choose PDFs or a whole folder from Files, Mail or the Share Sheet. Scanned pages are read with on-device text recognition.

  2. Read

    Dates and amounts are read exactly, never rounded. Pending and reverted payments are left out.

  3. Reconcile

    Opening balance plus every transaction must equal the closing balance. Anything that can’t be proven is flagged, not guessed.

  4. Export

    Review flagged rows, then export CSV, QIF, OFX, QFX, QBO or a PDF report.

Your statements stay yours

There’s no account to create and no KwiklyDoc server. Text extraction and the built-in parser run on your iPhone or iPad, and files are stored with iOS Data Protection and left out of backups.

AI help is optional, and KwiklyDoc asks before any statement text leaves your device.
